Attendance Risk Predictor — Full Product Specification
Product Overview
Attendance Risk Predictor is a daily-use web app for Indian college students. The student uploads their timetable once per semester, the app parses it using AI, and from that point forward automatically tracks how many classes of each subject have been conducted based on the calendar. The student periodically logs how many they attended or missed — whichever they remember. The app calculates current attendance %, risk level, safe bunks remaining, and suggests optimal days to skip around long weekends.
Core Value Proposition
For the student
Zero setup — one timetable upload
Always knows exact safe bunks left
Plans long weekends scientifically
Gets dramatic, funny warnings before it’s too late
Shareable result cards for WhatsApp / Instagram

Product moat
Lab vs lecture tracked separately
Holiday-aware auto-calculation
Bunk optimizer across subjects
One upload per semester — data persists
Semester history preserved on rollover

Complete User Flow
Onboarding (once per account)
1. Student visits the app and signs in with Google OAuth or magic link email. No password form.
2. Student is taken to semester setup screen.
3. Student picks semester term: Jan–May, Jul–Nov, or Custom date range.
4. Student uploads timetable: PDF, JPG, PNG, or Screenshot.
5. AI parses the timetable.
6. Student reviews extracted subjects on a correction screen.
7. Student marks holidays on a calendar.
8. Student enters initial attendance: Classes attended, Classes missed.
9. Student lands on the main dashboard.

Daily Use
- Open dashboard
- View risk scores
- Update attendance
- Simulate bunks
- Override conducted class count when required

Critical Edge Case
Colleges often reschedule classes or cancel lectures. Therefore: Conducted class override must be one tap away.

Feature Specification
Timetable Parser
Accepted Formats: PDF, JPG / PNG / WEBP, WhatsApp-compressed images, Rotated scans, Handwritten timetables.
Extracted Data: Subject name, Subject code, Session type (Lecture/Lab/Tutorial), Day of week, Time slot, Weekly frequency.

Risk Tier System
- Chill: Above 85% — “All good bhai.”
- Safe: 80–85% — “You’re fine.”
- Risky: 75–80% — “One bunk away from trouble.”
- Danger: 70–75% — “Ek aur bunk and you’re explaining yourself.”
- HOD Territory: Below 70% — “HOD is personally waiting.”

Bunk Planner
- Scenario Simulator: Pick future date, Simulate attendance impact.
- Long Weekend Detector: Detect Friday/Monday holidays, Predict 3–4 day weekends.
- AI Bunk Optimizer: Best skip plan for N days off.

Shareable Result Cards
- Meme-style cards, Instagram Story ratio (9:16), Randomized funny copy.
